  <h1>Artificial Human Design</h1>
  <p>Artificial Human Design (AHD) is a framework for creating design systems that prioritize quality and intentionality over AI-generated slop. We provide tools to compile briefs, lint designs, evaluate outcomes, and more.</p>

  <h2>Run the Eval</h2>
  <pre><code class="language-bash">ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models &lt;specs&gt; --n 10</code></pre>
