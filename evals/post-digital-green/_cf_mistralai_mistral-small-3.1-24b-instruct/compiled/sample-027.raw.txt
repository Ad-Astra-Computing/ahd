  <h1>ahd</h1>
  <p>Artificial Human Design is a framework for serious designers, engineers, and founders who want to take control of AI-generated design.</p>
  <p>We provide a set of tools to evaluate, lint, and critique design briefs, ensuring that the output is meaningful and aligned with your vision.</p>

  <h2>eval live</h2>
  <pre><code class="language-bash">ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models &lt;specs&gt; --n 10</code></pre>

  <h2>what ships</h2>
  <div class="manifest">
    <ul>
      <li>brief compiler</li>

      <li>eval harness</li>
      <li>vision-critic scaffold</li>
      <li>MCP server</li>
      <li>editor plugins</li>
      <li>eight tokens</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
