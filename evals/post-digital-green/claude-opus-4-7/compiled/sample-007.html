ahd · artificial human design · v0.1.0

a framework against design slop.

you already know the problem. another centred hero, another pill badge, another purple-to-blue wash on the word ai. ahd is the eval harness, linter and brief compiler that treats the slop thesis as a spec, not a vibe.

run one eval

one-liner. ten samples. compare specs against a compiled brief. 

#!/usr/bin/env bash
$ ahd eval-live swiss-editorial \
    --brief briefs/landing.yml \
    --models <specs> \
    --n 10
