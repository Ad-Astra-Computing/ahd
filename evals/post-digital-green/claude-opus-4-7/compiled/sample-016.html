ahd / artificial human design v0.1.0 · 2026-04-24

design tooling for people who are tired of generated slop. 

you have already noticed. every landing page rhymes. four-column footer, purple-to-blue hero, three equal cards, a pill badge, two ctas. ahd is the framework that makes a model defend its choices against a written brief, a 28-rule linter, and a vision critic — before you ever see the output.

run the eval

one-liner. ten samples. one brief. n model specs.

~/ahdbash
$ ahd eval-live swiss-editorial \
    --brief briefs/landing.yml \
    --models <specs> \
    --n 10
