    <div>
      <h3>thesis <span class="marg">01</span></h3>
      <p>design without constraint is statistically average. a model with no brief converges to the median of its training set. the cure is not a better prompt; it is a stricter brief and a harsher eval.</p>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h3>method <span class="marg">02</span></h3>
      <p>compile a human brief into a machine-readable spec. lint the output against 28 rules. run it through a vision critic. score, diff, iterate. no vibes.</p>
    </div>
  </section>

  <!-- rule: required ASCII diagram, rectangles only -->

  <h2 class="caps">what ships</h2>
  <p class="dim">in this repo, today, under source control, no keys required.</p>
  <ul class="manifest">
    <li><strong>brief compiler</strong> <span class="dim">— yml &rarr; typed spec, with forbidden-list resolution.</span></li>
    <li><strong>28-rule linter</strong> <span class="dim">— static checks against token and brief invariants.</span></li>
    <li><strong>eval harness</strong> <span class="dim">— n-sample runner, diff view, verdict ledger.</span></li>
    <li><strong>vision-critic scaffold</strong> <span class="dim">— prompt, rubric, and adapter shape (no keys wired).</span></li>
    <li><strong>mcp server</strong> <span class="dim">— exposes compiler + linter to local editor agents.</span></li>
    <li><strong>editor plugins</strong> <span class="dim">— source trees for vscode and zed, built from repo.</span></li>
    <li><strong>eight tokens</strong> <span class="dim">— post-digital-green, swiss-editorial, brutalist-zine, and five more.</span></li>
  </ul>
