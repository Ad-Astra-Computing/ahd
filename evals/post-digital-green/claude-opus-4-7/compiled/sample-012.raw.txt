    <h1>a framework for designers who can spot generated work<br>at twenty paces.</h1>
    <p style="margin-top:16px;max-width:72ch;"> <!-- rule: measure 72ch -->
      ahd is a brief compiler, a linter, and an eval harness. it converts a
      written design brief into machine-checkable rules, then scores model
      output against them. the thesis is unfashionable: that taste can be
      partially formalised, and that formalising it is cheaper than reviewing
      a thousand rounded-corner hero sections by hand.
    </p>
    <p class="dim" style="margin-top:12px;">
      no waitlist. no demo video. <a href="https://github.com/ahd/ahd" rel="noopener">read the code on github</a>
      &nbsp;or the&nbsp;<a href="https://codeberg.org/ahd/ahd" rel="noopener">forgejo mirror</a>.
    </p>
