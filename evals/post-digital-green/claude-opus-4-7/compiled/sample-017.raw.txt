<!-- rule: thesis without a lecture; audience already shares the priors -->
<section style="margin-top:16px;">
  <p>you already know what an llm-rendered landing page smells like. the<br>
  centred stack, the gradient on the word <span class="caps">ai</span>, three equal cards, the<br>
  trusted-by strip with logos nobody recognises. ahd is a small framework<br>
  for designers and engineers who want to put a stop to that without<br>
  going back to writing every grid by hand.</p>
  <p class="dim">it is a brief compiler, a linter, an eval harness and a critic. it<br>
  refuses what the token forbids before the model gets a turn.</p>
</section>

<!-- rule: terminal-aesthetic code block, lang tag bash, exact command -->
<section id="run" style="margin-top:16px;">
  <h2>run the eval</h2>
  <pre aria-label="bash"><span class="lang">bash</span><span class="prompt">$ </span>ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models &lt;specs&gt; --n 10<span class="caret">&nbsp;</span></pre>
  <p class="dim" style="margin-top:8px;">runs the brief against n=10 candidates per model, lints each, and<br>
  scores the screenshots. exits non-zero on any forbidden-token violation.</p>
</section>
