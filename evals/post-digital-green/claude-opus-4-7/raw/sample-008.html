A linter and eval harness for the generated web.

You already know what the slop looks like: the purple hero, the three equal cards, the pill badge above two buttons, the same testimonial grid in three different fonts. AHD is the part that treats that pattern as a measurable defect and grades against it.

AHD compiles a written brief into constraints, lints the output against twenty-eight rules derived from Swiss editorial practice, and scores candidates from live models with a vision critic. No vibe-driven review, no screenshot-Twitter consensus. A committed brief, a deterministic grader, a diff you can argue with.

Run the grader yourself:

~ / ahd bash 
$ ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10

Ten candidates per model, graded against briefs/landing.yml, diffed against the 28-rule baseline, dropped into a scoreboard you can check into git. The brief is the contract. Everything else is evidence.

What is gated — on external resources

    Live frontier-model calls The eval-live path requires your own API keys. Stub adapters run offline against recorded fixtures.
    gated
  • 09
    Live vision critique Needs a multimodal key and a screenshot pipeline (Playwright or equivalent). Scaffold is in-tree; the critic is not wired to anything by default.
    gated
