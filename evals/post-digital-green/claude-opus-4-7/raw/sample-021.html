AHD / ARTIFICIAL HUMAN DESIGN
eval ships gated source

A framework for design that has read the room on AI slop.

AHD is a brief compiler, a linter, and an eval harness. It produces a Swiss-editorial artefact from a YAML brief, lints it against 28 rules a working designer would already notice, and scores it across a panel of models so the output is reviewable instead of vibes.

It is not a generator dressed in a serif font. The generator is the cheap part; the linter and the eval are the point.

You have seen the pattern: pill badge, two CTAs, three equal cards, purple-to-blue gradient, a testimonial that no human wrote. AHD treats that pattern as a failure mode, not an aesthetic.

The rules are legible. The eval is reproducible. Disagree with a rule, delete it; the harness still runs.

Run the eval reproducible · local

~/ahd bash 
$ ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10

Ten generations per model, linted, scored by the vision critic when a multimodal key is present, diffed against the brief. Exit code is non-zero if the 28-rule linter fails. CI-friendly.
