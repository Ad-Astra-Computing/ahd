AHD// artificial human design
v0.4.2 · commit 8ad1f30 · 2026-04-24

A framework for design that doesn't read as slop.

AHD is a compiler, a linter, and an eval harness for layout. You write a brief; it produces candidates under a Swiss-editorial grammar; a 28-rule linter rejects the obvious tells; a vision critic scores what's left. You already know why this matters.

No generative mysticism. The output is deterministic given the brief, the tokens, and the seed. Models are graded, not trusted.

One command, ten samples, graded against the brief.

bash
$ ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
requires AHD_OPENAI_KEY, AHD_ANTHROPIC_KEY, or equivalent in env. dry-run works offline.
02 · what shipstoday, in the repo

What ships

brief compiler
YAML → typed intermediate representation; enforces constraint vocabulary before anything reaches a model.
28-rule linter
Catches centred hero stacks, pill-badge duplets, three equal feature cards, gradient-on-AI, shimmer, and 23 other known tells.
eval harness
Deterministic runs with seeds; N-sample comparisons; structured JSON + markdown reports.
vision-critic scaffold
Pluggable grader interface with a local heuristic baseline; wired for multimodal backends.
MCP server
Exposes the compiler, linter, and harness to any MCP-speaking editor or agent over stdio and SSE.
editor plugins
VS Code and Zed extensions, shipped in-tree, installable from source.
eight tokens
The starter token set: two typefaces, one scale, two neutrals, one accent, one rule, one measure. CC-BY-4.0.

AHD is an opinion, compiled. The opinion is that generative systems, left to their defaults, regress toward the mean of last year's landing pages, and the mean is bad.

If you disagree, the linter rules are in rules/. Fork it.
