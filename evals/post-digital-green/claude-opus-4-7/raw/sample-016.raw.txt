  <h1>A framework for treating <em>generated design</em> as a first-class artefact — and grading it.</h1>

  <p class="lede">
    You have already noticed the pattern: the same hero, the same gradient, the
    same three cards, the same purple. AHD does not argue with that diagnosis.
    AHD ships the linter, the brief compiler, and the eval harness that let you
    measure how often a model produces it — and how to push the score down.
  </p>

  <p>
    The thesis is small. Most generated layouts fail not because the model
    cannot draw, but because nothing in the loop is willing to call a layout
    derivative. AHD is that thing. It is a Swiss-editorial style guide
    expressed as 28 lint rules, a set of eight typographic and spatial tokens,
    and a vision-critic that judges renderings against the brief that produced
    them.
  </p>
