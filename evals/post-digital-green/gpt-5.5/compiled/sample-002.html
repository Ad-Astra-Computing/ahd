AHD/Artificial Human Design

design evals for generated interfaces

AHD turns a brief into constraints, lint rules, model runs, and review artifacts. it treats taste as inspectable surface area: typography, grid, colour, spacing, motion, assets, forbidden tropes, and the gap between what was asked and what rendered.
