artificial human design / ahd

AHD takes generated design seriously.

AHD compiles briefs, checks output against design rules, and runs repeatable evals so teams can argue from artifacts instead of taste. It is small, inspectable, and meant for working designers, senior engineers, and founders.

read-the-code CTA

run the eval

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
