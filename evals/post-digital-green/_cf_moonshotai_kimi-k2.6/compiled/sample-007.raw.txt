    <section>
        <p>you have seen the slop. generic gradients, purple-to-blue heroes, three identical feature cards, testimonials from people who do not exist. you do not need another lecture about why this is bad.</p>
        
        <p>what you need is a framework that treats design as a discipline with rules, constraints, and evaluation. AHD is that framework. it compiles briefs into spec, lints output against 28 rules, and runs eval harnesses against frontier models.</p>

        <p>the thesis is simple: generative tools are infrastructure, not authors. the author is still the human with taste. AHD enforces that boundary.</p>
    </section>
