    <h1>design is not a prompt. it is a contract.</h1>
    
    <p>you have seen the slop. thin gradients, three identical cards, a "trusted by" bar of logos nobody recognises. you do not need another lecture on why this happens.</p>
    
    <p>ahd is a framework for evaluating design outputs against structured briefs. it treats generated work as a compile target and checks it like one.</p>
