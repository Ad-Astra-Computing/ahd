AHD — v0.1.0 — framework for evaluating design output

You have seen the slop. You do not need the lecture. This is a framework for running structured evaluations against generated design, with rules you can read and change.

AHD treats the brief as code, the linter as policy, and the eval as reproducible. No magic. No black box scoring. You write the rules, you pick the models, you inspect the results.
