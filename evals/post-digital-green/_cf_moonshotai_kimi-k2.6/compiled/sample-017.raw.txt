    <h1>artificial human design</h1>

    <p>you have seen the slop. the slop sees you back. this is a framework for evaluating whether generated design meets a standard — any standard you define — before it ships.</p>

    <p>AHD is not a design tool. it is a protocol: brief in, scored artefact out. the brief is compiled to a prompt, the output is linted against rules, critiqued by vision models, and ranked. nothing ships unexamined.</p>
