<h1>artificial human design</h1>

<p>you have seen the slop. the over-warmed layouts, the confidence-masked mediocrity, the design that reads as first-draft because that is exactly what it is.</p>

<p>ahd does not sell you on the problem. ahd gives you a framework to evaluate, constrain, and improve machine-generated design before it reaches a user.</p>

<p>the thesis is simple: <span style="color: oklch(0.82 0.15 75);">ai is a material, not a method</span>. treat it like one.</p>

<hr> <!-- rule: hairline rule colour -->

<pre><code class="language-bash">ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models &lt;specs&gt; --n 10</code></pre> <!-- rule: required bash code block -->

<p class="dim">run evaluations against named design systems with configurable model pools. the harness scores output against a compiled brief and reports variance across runs.</p>
