artificial human design

v0.1.0 — a framework for evaluating generative design output

you have seen the slop. flat purple gradients. three identical cards. a centered pill badge with two ctas. corporate memphis waving from every hero section.

ahd does not explain why this happens. you already know. ahd provides the tooling to catch it before it ships: a brief compiler that turns intent into constraints, a linter with twenty-eight rules derived from actual design failure modes, and an eval harness that runs models against those constraints at scale. 

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
