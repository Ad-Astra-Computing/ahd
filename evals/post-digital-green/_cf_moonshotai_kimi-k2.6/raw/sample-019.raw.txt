        <div class="container">
            <div class="mark">Artificial Human Design</div>
            <h1>A framework for evaluating design systems, not generating them</h1>
            <p class="lead">
                You have already seen what happens when layout, colour, and typography are
                outsourced to pattern completion. <strong>AHD treats design quality as a
                verifiable property</strong> — measured against explicit rules, comparable
                across model outputs, and inspectable in code.
            </p>
            <p class="lead">
                This is not a tool that writes CSS for you. It is a toolchain that lets you
                state what good design means for your context, run models against that
                definition, and choose whether the output meets your bar.
            </p>
        </div>
    </header>
