            <section class="thesis">
                <p>You have already seen the problem. Templates regenerated at scale. Hero sections that all share the same five decisions. Copy that reads like latents interpolated between forty landing pages. The issue is not that tools exist; it is that most workflows have no mechanism for taste.</p>
                <p><strong>AHD is an evaluation framework, not a generator.</strong> You write a brief in structured YAML. The compiler turns it into a rubric. A 28-rule linter catches patterns that correlate with genericoutput. An eval harness runs your design candidates against models you choose. A vision-critic scaffold lets multimodal models inspect rendered output when you have keys. The result is scored output you can argue with, reground, and version.</p>
                <p class="muted">This is for teams who already have designers and already distrust the average case. It gives you vocabulary, guardrails, and a loop.</p>
            </section>
