          <span class="tagline">Artificial HumanDesign</span>
        </div>
        <div class="problem-statement">
          <p>Every AI-adjacent tool promises to eliminate taste from the work. We take the opposite position: design evaluation is a teachable skill, and the cadence of doing it well can be compressed without losing rigor.</p>
          <p>AHD is a toolkit for running structured evaluations of generative design output. It does not generate. It measures, compares, and documents what made it through the filter and what did not.</p>
        </div>
