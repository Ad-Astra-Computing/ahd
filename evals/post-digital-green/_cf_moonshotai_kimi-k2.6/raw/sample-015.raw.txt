<section class="hero">
  <h1>A framework for evaluating design systems against human criteria, not engagement metrics.</h1>
  <p>Most AI-generated interfaces fail the same way: they look finished but behave like prototypes. Layouts collapse under real content. Color relationships don't survive accessibility filters. Interaction states are omitted because the model wasn't asked.</p>
  <p>AHD treats these as measurable failures. It provides a structured brief compiler, a 28-rule linter with visual semantics, and an evaluation harness that scores output against the constraints you actually defined—not the ones the model inferred.</p>
  <p>The system is self-contained for audit and reproducibility. Nothing phones home. Frontier model calls and screenshot vision critique are gated behind your own keys, which means you control the cost surface and the data surface.</p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2>Run an evaluation</h2>
  <p>Evaluations are terminal-first. The harness reads your brief, compiles constraints, runs the candidate through the linter, and produces scored output with traceable rule violations.</p>
  
  <div class="code-block">
    <div class="code-header">
      <span class="lang-tag">bash</span>
      <button class="copy-btn" onclick="navigator.clipboard.writeText(this.closest('.code-block').querySelector('pre').innerText)">Copy</button>
    </div>
    <pre>ahd eval-live swiss-editorial <span class="flag">\</span>
  <span class="flag">--brief</span> <span class="arg">briefs/landing.yml</span> <span class="flag">\</span>
  <span class="flag">--models</span> <span class="value">&lt;specs&gt;</span> <span class="flag">\</span>
  <span class="flag">--n</span> <span class="value">10</span></pre>
  </div>
  
  <p class="note">Replace <code>&lt;specs&gt;</code> with provider model identifiers (e.g. <code>anthropic/claude-opus-4</code>, <code>openai/gpt-4o</code>). The harness dispatches to your configured endpoints. No proxy, no quota pool.</p>
</section>
