<header>
    <div class="sigil"><span>~/ahd</span> v0.9.0</div>
    <h1>A framework for evaluating whether generated interfaces hold up to human standards.</h1>
    <div class="lead">
        <p>Every AI-generated site looks plausible at first glance and falls apart on the third click. AHD is a toolchain for running that third click systematically: compile a brief, lint against a fixed rule set, score against a vision model, and keep a record of what failed and why.</p>
        <p>It is not a component library. It is not a no-code builder. It is a method for turning taste into testable constraints.</p>
    </div>
</header>
