<header>
  <h1>Artificial Human Design</h1>
  <p>You already suspect the problem. The homogenized layouts, the predictable component structures and the thoughtless aesthetic gestures of generated interfaces have saturated the web.</p>
  <p>AHD is a design framework built to enforce discipline on model outputs. Rather than writing subjective prompts, we impose deterministic linting, hard stylistic constraints and visual evaluation pipelines to demand intent.</p>
</header>
