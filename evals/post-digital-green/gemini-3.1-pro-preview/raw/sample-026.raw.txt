    <h1>Artificial Human Design</h1>
    <p>You have seen the state of generative layouts. You know the homogenous structures, the generic aesthetic and the synthetic polish that dominate the landscape. AHD offers no lectures on the matter: it provides a rigorous framework to correct it.</p>
    <p>This is a toolset designed to constrain stochastic models. It enforces typographic discipline, eliminates unpredictable layouts and produces testable artifacts that respect strict constraints.</p>
    
    <pre><code class="language-bash">ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models &lt;specs&gt; --n 10</code></pre>

    <h2>Manifest</h2>
    
    <h3>What ships</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>brief compiler</li>
      <li>28-rule linter</li>
      <li>eval harness</li>
      <li>vision-critic scaffold</li>
      <li>MCP server</li>
      <li>editor plugins</li>
      <li>eight tokens</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>What is gated</h3>
    <ul>

      <li>standalone npm packages for the editor plugins</li>
      <li>additional tokens</li>
    </ul>
