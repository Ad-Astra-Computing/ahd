    <p>
      You already know what generated layouts look like. They abandon structural constraint, discard typographic hierarchy and rely on superficial styling to mask fundamental failures in logic. 
    </p>
    <p>
      AHD is an enforcement layer. It imposes deterministic boundaries and formal rulesets on layout synthesis. It is a strictly structured framework for engineers and designers who require absolute control over visual output rather than settling for stochastic approximation.
    </p>
