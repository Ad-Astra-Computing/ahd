  <h1>Artificial Human Design</h1>

  <p>You have seen the state of generated interfaces over the last month. The default output is unstructured, illegible and detached from intent. AHD (Artificial Human Design) is a framework that takes the problem of slop seriously. It provides the leverage to force large language models into rendering structurally sound code.</p>

  <p>Instead of writing open-ended prompts, you compile rigid briefs. You evaluate outputs against a strict ruleset. You rely on mechanical linting and vision-critic scaffolds to enforce baseline layout invariants. AHD provides the harness. You provide the constraints.</p>

  <div class="terminal">
    <pre><code class="language-bash">ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models &lt;specs&gt; --n 10</code></pre>
  </div>
