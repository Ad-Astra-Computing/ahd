Artificial Human Design

The web is drowning in synthetic slop. Every generated interface converges on the same mean. You already know this. You do not need a lecture on why the current state of output is unacceptable.

Artificial Human Design (AHD) is not an assistant. It is a strict evaluation and linting harness for interface generation. It forces models to respect structural constraints, typographic discipline and spatial logic. It provides the framework to encode aesthetic taste into programmable rules.

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
