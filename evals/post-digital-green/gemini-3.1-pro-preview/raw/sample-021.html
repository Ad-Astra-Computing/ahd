AHD

You have seen the synthetic output flooding the network. It lacks intent, constraint and taste. AHD does not attempt to lecture the industry on the mechanics of slop. It simply provides the primitives to prevent it.

Artificial Human Design is a framework for enforcing strict typographic and structural constraints on generative interfaces. It replaces speculative prompting with deterministic evaluation and programmatic linting. The focus is exclusively on the final render.

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
