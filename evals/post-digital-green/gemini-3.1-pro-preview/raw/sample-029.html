Artificial Human Design

You have seen the generated output of the last month. You already suspect the problem. Automated web generation defaults to indistinct, redundant and functionally fragile patterns. AHD provides a structural framework to enforce discipline on synthetic generation. We treat design constraints as compile-time errors.
