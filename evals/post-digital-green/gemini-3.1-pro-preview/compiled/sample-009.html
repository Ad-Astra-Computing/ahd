artificial human design (ahd)

generative outputs default to slop. you already suspect this. ahd is a rigid, post-digital framework to enforce spatial and typographic intent mathematically. it rejects the hallucination cycle by asserting a strict compilation and evaluation phase over model responses.

we do not generate components; we compile briefs against an immutable set of rules. if the output violates the char grid, fails the contrast tier, or introduces forbidden patterns, the linter fails it.

[ read-the-code ] 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
