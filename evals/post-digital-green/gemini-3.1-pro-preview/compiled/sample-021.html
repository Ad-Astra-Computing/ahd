ARTIFICIAL HUMAN DESIGN

the internet, today, is awash in slop. you know it. we know it. artificial human design (ahd) is a framework to produce exceptional, brief-driven user interfaces. it formalizes the ephemeral into hard rules, generating creative variations while ensuring adherence to specific design tokens and constraints.

|  [design intent, audience, surfaces]                                   |
|  [style direction, forbidden list, must-haves]                         |

| DESIGN OUTPUT -> HTML, CSS, ASSETS                                     |
|  [valid, compliant, unique, on-brief]                                  |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------+

ahd orchestrates models, tools, and human expertise to solve for the specific rather than the generic. we provide the framework for design automation that respects craft.

EVALUATE CANDIDATE DESIGNS

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
