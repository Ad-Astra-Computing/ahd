artificial human design

the problem is clear: too much ai-generated output lacks rigor and specificity. it's not just a quality issue; it's a fidelity problem. the internet is filling with indistinct artifacts, blurring the lines between signal and noise. this isn't a critique of ai, but of its uncalibrated application.

ahd is a framework for calibrating ai for design tasks. it offers a structured approach to defining, evaluating, and refining ai-generated designs against human-centric criteria. we're not chasing faster slop, but meaningful, measurable results. a framework, not a lecture.

  <brief> --> [compiler] --> {model query}
                           /|\
                            |
                          [eval] <-- {model output}
                           \|/
                            |
                          [critic] --> <report>

test how well a prompt performs against a specific brief. then iterate.

run an evaluation

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
