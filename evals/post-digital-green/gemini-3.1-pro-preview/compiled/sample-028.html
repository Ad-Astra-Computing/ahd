AHD._

the flood of synthetic design is here. you've seen it. you've dismissed it. but it's not going away. artificial human design (ahd) is a framework to tame the deluge, not to escape it. we give you the tools to integrate agentic outputs into your workflow, with precision, control, and a rigorous quality baseline.

ahd is not about replacing human creativity. it's about augmenting it, providing a consistent system for evaluating and refining agent-generated design. we turn slop into signal.
