        </p>
        <div class="summary">
          <p>
            The point is not another gallery of machine-made landing pages. The point is a repeatable way to compile a brief, lint it against concrete rules, run comparative evals, and keep a record of what was actually judged.
          </p>
          <p>
            One command, one surface, one manifest. What runs locally is separated from what still depends on external model access.
          </p>
