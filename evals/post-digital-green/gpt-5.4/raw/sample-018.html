AHD
Artificial Human Design Frameworks for evaluating generated design against an actual brief
Read the code
One brief. Ten runs. Visible criteria.

Less style theater. More design pressure.

AHD treats generated design as something to inspect, constrain, and compare, not something to romanticize. If you already know the problem, this is the practical layer: compile the brief, lint the output, run the eval, inspect what passed, and keep the parts that survive contact with taste and implementation reality.

For designers Bring the brief back to the front of the process instead of reverse-engineering intent from polished noise.
For engineers and founders See what is executable today, what still depends on outside infrastructure, and where the system fails in plain view.
