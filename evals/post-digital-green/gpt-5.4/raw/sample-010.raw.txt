          <div class="eyebrow">A framework against design slop</div>
          <h1 id="hero-title">Make generated design answerable.</h1>
          <p class="lede">
            AHD is for people who already know the failure mode: polished noise, vague taste, no audit trail. The point is not to generate more pages. The point is to impose structure, run the eval, and inspect what survives contact with a real brief.
          </p>
          <p class="thesis">
            The thesis is simple: if a system cannot state its constraints, expose its checks, and show what is actually implemented versus merely promised, it is not a design workflow. It is a demo.
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="hero-side">
          <aside class="panel terminal" aria-label="Eval command">
            <div class="terminal-head">
              <span>live-eval.sh</span>
              <span>one line, inspectable</span>
            </div>
            <pre><code class="language-bash">ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models &lt;specs&gt; --n 10</code></pre>
          </aside>
          <div class="note">
            The command is the product surface: a named mode, a concrete brief, explicit model specs, and a finite sample count. No hidden taste engine. No mystery score.
          </div>
        </div>
