          <h1 id="hero-title">Stop calling decorative drift a design system.</h1>
          <p class="lede">
            AHD treats AI-generated interfaces as something to inspect, constrain, lint, and evaluate. Not admire.
            The point is not another generator. The point is a way to tell whether the output deserved to exist.
          </p>
          <div class="hero-notes">
            <div class="note">
              Working designers get a brief compiler and tokens that push style decisions back into explicit language.
            </div>

            </div>
          </div>
        </div>
