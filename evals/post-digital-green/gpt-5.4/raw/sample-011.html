Framework over slogans

Design that survives contact with an eval.

AHD is for people who can already spot generated sameness on first scroll. It treats that failure mode as a systems problem: compile the brief, lint the work, run the batch, inspect the misses, and make the design answerable to something stricter than taste alone. No lecture. No theater. A concrete loop.
