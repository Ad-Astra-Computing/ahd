A framework for catching design slop before it ships.

AHD does not argue that the web has become interchangeable. It assumes you have already seen it. The point is operational: compile the brief, lint the output, run the eval, and make the failure modes visible enough to fix.

eval-live.sh
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
Why This Exists

Most generated sites fail in ways that are obvious to anyone who has had to defend a system, a launch, or a design review. They flatten voice, repeat patterns, overfit to trends, and hide weak judgment behind smooth surfaces.

AHD turns that suspicion into a repeatable check: a brief format, a rule set, an eval harness, and editor tooling that make quality legible.

Method

This is not a gallery of prompts and not a lecture about taste. It is a working constraint system for teams that need to inspect generated design with the same seriousness they apply to code.

Use Case

Working designers can use it to make briefs less vague. Engineers can wire it into review loops. Founders can stop debating whether something feels generic and start naming why.
