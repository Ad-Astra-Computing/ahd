Design evals, not design slogans

If it looks generated, it probably was not judged hard enough.

AHD turns vague disgust into a repeatable review loop: compile the brief, lint the output, run multiple candidates, critique what the model made, and keep only work that survives contact with a human standard.

This is not a sermon about slop. It is a working surface for designers, engineers, and founders who need a framework they can inspect, run, and extend.

  • Briefs first The system starts from explicit constraints instead of post-hoc excuses for generic output.
  • Visible criteria The linter and eval harness make judgment legible enough to argue with, improve, and rerun.
  • Present-tense tools The manifest below separates what ships today from what depends on external model access or packaging work.
