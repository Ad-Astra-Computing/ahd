AHD / Artificial Human Design
Framework, not lecture.

Less output. More taste under pressure.

AHD is for people who already know the failure mode: polished sameness, synthetic confidence, no editorial spine. It turns that instinct into a runnable brief, a linter, and an eval path you can inspect instead of a vibe you have to argue about.

The point is not to automate judgment. The point is to make judgment legible, repeatable, and difficult to hand-wave away.

Read the code One screen. No slogan inflation. No decorative certainty.
eval-live.sh terminal / runnable 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
