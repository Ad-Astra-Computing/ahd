AHD / Artificial Human Design
A framework for people who already know the failure mode: vague prompts, generic taste, no constraints, no audit trail.

Less generated. More designed.

AHD treats AI output as something to evaluate, constrain, and inspect rather than admire on first render. The brief is explicit. The rules are visible. The artifacts are testable. What survives is closer to editorial judgment than autocomplete.

eval-live 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10

Read the system

This is not a brand story wrapped around a prompt box. It is a set of opinions, tools, and checks for teams who want repeatable visual standards without accepting default model taste.

read-the-code

What ships

Available now in the working surface.

  • brief compilerTurns intent into a concrete, inspectable spec.
  • 28-rule linterRejects familiar slop patterns before they harden into output.
  • eval harnessRuns comparative generations and records the result set.
  • vision-critic scaffoldStructured hooks for image-based review and scoring.
  • MCP serverExposes the system to editors and agent workflows.
  • editor pluginsIn-editor access for the environments people actually use.
  • eight tokensA constrained starting vocabulary, not an infinitely expandable moodboard.

What is gated

Available when external dependencies are present.

  • live frontier-model callsNeeds API keys.
  • live vision critiqueNeeds multimodal key + screenshot pipeline.
  • standalone npm packages for the editor pluginsPackaging and distribution are separated from the core repo.
  • additional tokensHeld back until they earn their place in the system.
