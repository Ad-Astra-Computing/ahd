AHD / Artificial Human Design
A framework for evaluating whether a design still looks like someone meant it.
Read the code

Design evaluation for people who can already spot the pattern.

AHD is not another generator dressed up as taste. It turns a brief into a checkable standard, runs work against it, and makes the failure modes visible. The point is not more output. The point is a usable way to reject the familiar, convenient drift toward generic AI layouts.

Why this exists

Designers, engineers, and founders do not need another sermon about slop. They need a repeatable eval, a linter with teeth, and a manifest that says what is real right now.

One screen. One command. One honest boundary between what ships and what still depends on outside infrastructure.
Live Eval 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
Working premise

If a system cannot explain why a page feels templated, it will keep reproducing the same safe, polished mistakes. AHD makes the rules explicit enough to audit, argue with, and improve.

Read-the-code CTA

The implementation matters more than the moodboard. Use the repository as the product surface: inspect the rules, the harness, and the plugin path before you decide whether the framework earns a place in your workflow.
