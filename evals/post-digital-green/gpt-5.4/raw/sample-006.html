Artificial Human Design
Not another AI landing page. AHD is the tooling layer for teams that want taste, scrutiny, and failure modes they can inspect.

A framework, not a sermon

Design work deserves an eval, not a vibe.

AHD turns the anti-slop position into a working system: compile the brief, lint the output, run the eval, inspect the misses, and keep the parts that still require a human eye plainly visible.

For people who read repos before decks

The point is not to make machine output look expensive. The point is to force a standard that generic generation cannot bluff its way past.

eval-live / swiss-editorial
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10

What the command does

It runs the brief against a concrete visual standard instead of asking for generic “quality.” You get variance, comparison, and something testable.

Why it matters

Teams already know the failure pattern: plausible copy, interchangeable layouts, surface polish, no judgment. AHD treats that as an engineering problem.
