ahd / artificial human design / eval-driven design systems

less synthetic polish. more accountable output.

ahd treats generated design as something to inspect, score, and constrain. the point is not more surface area. the point is a brief, a linter, a repeatable eval, and a record of what failed.

for designers, engineers, and founders who already know the smell of generic output, this is the working frame: make taste explicit, test it, and keep the artefacts visible.
