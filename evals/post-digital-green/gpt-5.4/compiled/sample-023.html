artificial human design / landing / v0.1

ai output is easy to spot. ahd treats that as a bug.

ahd is a framework for writing stricter briefs, linting them against explicit rules, and running comparative evals before a generated page reaches production. the point is not taste theatre. the point is to remove the recurring failure modes and leave a readable trail.

if you already know what slop looks like, this is the layer that makes the model answer to something more concrete than preference.
