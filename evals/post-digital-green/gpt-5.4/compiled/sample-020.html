ahd / artificial human design
token: post-digital-green v0.1.0

design systems for people who are already tired of generated design.

ahd is a framework for writing briefs, linting outputs, and running repeatable visual evals against actual style rules. the point is not to lecture you about slop. the point is to make it measurable.
