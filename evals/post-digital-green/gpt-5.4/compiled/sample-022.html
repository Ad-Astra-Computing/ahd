artificial human design / ahd post-digital-green v0.1.0

a framework for catching generated sameness before it passes for taste.

ahd compiles briefs into constraints, lints outputs against explicit rules, and runs evals that make visual drift legible. this is not a lecture about slop. it is a way to inspect it.

the point is simple: if the brief matters, the failure modes need names, checks, and a repeatable harness.
