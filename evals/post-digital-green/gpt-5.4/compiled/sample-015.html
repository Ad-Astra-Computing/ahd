artificial human design / web / v0.1

ahd is a framework for catching the parts of generated design that feel pre-solved.

briefs become constraints. constraints become checks. checks become a repeatable eval you can run against generated surfaces before they harden into a house style nobody chose.

for designers, engineers, and founders who are done mistaking output volume for taste.

the point is not to automate taste. the point is to make taste legible enough to test, fail, and revise in public.

run the eval against a live brief:

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10

today, ahd is opinionated on purpose: explicit briefs, visible rules, inspectable outputs, and enough tooling to keep generated sameness from passing as design work.
