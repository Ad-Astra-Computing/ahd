Artificial Human Design (AHD)

For designers, engineers, and founders who have seen the AI‑generated noise and need a framework that actually evaluates and improves output, AHD provides a concrete, open‑source toolchain.

Run the evaluation

Copy the one‑liner into your terminal:

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10

  • eval harness
