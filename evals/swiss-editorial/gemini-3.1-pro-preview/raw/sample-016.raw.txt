      <p>The web is saturated with generated interfaces. You recognize the signatures: homogenous layout patterns, indiscriminate structural decisions and a systemic reversion to the mean. The problem is not generation itself. The problem is generating without constraint, critique or intent.</p>
      <p>AHD is a deterministic framework for interface generation. It replaces conversational prompting with a strict editorial pipeline. It enforces architectural rules, lints output against defined design principles and evaluates results continuously. It is built for teams who require a compiler for design rather than a chatbot.</p>
