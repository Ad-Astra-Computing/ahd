  <h1>An evaluation framework for deterministic design</h1>
  
  <p>You already know the problem. The generated output is a blur of lowest-common-denominator decisions and lazy abstractions. AHD is an assertion of control over these pipelines. It replaces generic prompting with a strict evaluation harness and a robust set of deterministic constraints.</p>

  <p>The framework operates as a local utility. You define the brief and the exact model specifications. AHD handles the compilation, applies the rules, and runs the evaluation loop to ensure the output aligns with your baseline.</p>

  <div class="code-container">
<pre><code class="language-bash">ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models &lt;specs&gt; --n 10</code></pre>
  </div>
