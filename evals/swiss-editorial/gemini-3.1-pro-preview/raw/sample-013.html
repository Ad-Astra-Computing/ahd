Artificial Human Design (AHD)

Structural design evaluation framework.

The web has been saturated with generated interfaces that lack intent, coherence, and taste. AHD assumes you already recognise the degradation of structural design quality. It provides a programmatic framework to measure, critique, and correct output, enforcing rigorous constraints over default aesthetic compromises.

This is a practical toolset for evaluating models against deterministic rules and specific design briefs, isolating true capability from probabilistic styling.
