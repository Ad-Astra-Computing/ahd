Artificial Human Design

Read the code

The web is drowning in extruded, pattern-matched noise. We have traded intention for volume, generating interfaces that look correct from a distance but disintegrate under scrutiny. You already suspect the problem. You do not need to be sold on the slop thesis.

AHD is an engineering framework for interface constraint. It forces generative models to output structured and evaluable design tokens instead of hallucinated DOM trees. It measures spatial rhythm, typographical hierarchy and structural integrity against strict rulesets.

We do not generate pages. We generate briefs, compile them into constraints, force models into a narrow band of acceptable outputs and brutally evaluate the variance. It is a system for asserting human intent over statistical drift. 

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10

What ships

  • eval harness
  • vision-critic scaffold
