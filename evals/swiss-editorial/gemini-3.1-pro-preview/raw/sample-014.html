AHD

Artificial Human Design

The web is drowning in generated output. You already know the exact patterns: the identical layouts, the predictable phrasing, the fundamental lack of structural rigour. If you suspect generation models have compromised design standards, you are correct.

AHD is a framework built to enforce strict, deterministic design systems against non-deterministic outputs. It evaluates, lints and compiles design intent before any pixels render. This is not a lecture on aesthetics. This is the mechanism to enforce them.

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
