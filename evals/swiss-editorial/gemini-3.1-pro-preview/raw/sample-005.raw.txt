  <h1>AHD : Artificial Human Design</h1>

  <p>The output is a known quantity. The default generation patterns are unacceptable. You do not need a lecture on the state of the industry; you need a mechanical way to enforce rules. AHD is a strict evaluation and linting framework designed to override defaults and guarantee structural design intent.</p>

  <p>Run the evaluation suite locally to verify the delta against baseline models.</p>

  <div class="terminal">
    <pre><code class="language-bash">ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models &lt;specs&gt; --n 10</code></pre>
  </div>
