Artificial Human Design

You have seen the output. The predictable components, the generic layouts and the total absence of friction. The web is flooded with synthetic design slop.

AHD provides a framework to measure, lint and enforce design intent against generative models. We treat design as code, evaluations as unit tests and the output as something that must be strictly governed rather than passively accepted.

This is a framework, not a lecture.

What is gated

  • live vision critique (needs multimodal key + screenshot pipeline)
