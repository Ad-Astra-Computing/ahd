Artificial Human Design

The web is saturated with generated aesthetic noise. We do not need another tool to produce untethered interface elements. We need a systematic framework to mechanically constrain, evaluate and refine them.

AHD provides a deterministic harness that replaces subjective prompts with rigid design invariants. It forces models to respect human-authored rules before pixels ever reach a surface.

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
