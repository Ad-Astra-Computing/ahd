      <p>You have seen the output. The generic layouts, the predictable structures and the sheer volume of uninspired interfaces generated over the last month. The problem is not the models. The problem is the absence of systemic constraints.</p>
      
      <p>AHD is a rigorous framework for enforcing typographic discipline, spatial logic and intentional design choices on generated output. It replaces vague prompting with explicit directives and measurable evaluations. We do not generate interfaces: we compile briefs, lint against strict rulesets and execute evaluations.</p>
