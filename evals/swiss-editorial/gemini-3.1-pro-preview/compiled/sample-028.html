Artificial Human Design

Generation defaults to slop. Unconstrained tooling produces average aesthetics. AHD imposes rigid limits to restore typographic discipline. You provide the brief. The compiler enforces the rules. It refuses noise and default gradients.

We rely on strict evaluation rather than vague prompting. Output is scored against objective parameters. It runs locally.

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
