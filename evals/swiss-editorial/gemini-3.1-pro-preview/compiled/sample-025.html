Framework.

Generative models default to predictable outputs. They hallucinate rounded corners, soft shadows and generic layouts. You already know the problem.

Artificial Human Design enforces typographic discipline. It compiles rules, lints output and penalizes failures. No gradient text. No structural ambiguity. Pure deterministic constraint.

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
