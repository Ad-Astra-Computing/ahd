artificial human design

No more slop

AHD is a framework for evaluating design output from frontier models. It compiles briefs into structured specifications, lints against explicit rules, and scores results with repeatable rigour.

Run the eval

One command. Ten variations. One brief.

ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
