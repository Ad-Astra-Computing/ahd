            </h1>

            <div class="lede">
                <p>
                    AHD is an evaluation framework for interface design. You write a brief in YAML, run it against models you already have access to, and get back a scored, ranked, linted set of candidates—with explicit failure modes, not vibes.
                </p>
                <p>
                    It does not generate. It does not replace your judgment. It makes your judgment reproducible.
                </p>
