<section class="wrap" style="border-bottom: none;">
  <h2>Operating principles</h2>
  <ul class="principles">
    <li>
      <span class="principle-num">01</span>
      <p class="principle-title">Evaluation over generation</p>
      <p class="principle-body">AHD does not make design. It makes design accountable to criteria that were written before the prompt.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="principle-num">02</span>
      <p class="principle-title">Rules before models</p>
      <p class="principle-body">The 28-rule linter runs offline, deterministic, no API call. Models are for judgment calls the rules cannot reach.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="principle-num">03</span>
      <p class="principle-title">Brief as contract</p>
      <p class="principle-body">A brief is not a prompt. It is a structured document: constraints, references, anti-examples, success criteria.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="principle-num">04</span>
      <p class="principle-title">Human in the loop, not on the ladder</p>
      <p class="principle-body">The framework automates ranking and surfaces disagreement. The designer decides.</p>
    </li>
  </ul>
</section>
