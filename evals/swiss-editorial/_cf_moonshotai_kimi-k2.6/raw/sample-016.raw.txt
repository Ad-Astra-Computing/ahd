            <h1>A framework for evaluating design without generating it.</h1>

            <div class="lede">
                <p>Most AI design tools produce slop because they optimize for speed, not coherence. AHD does not generate layouts. It evaluates them—against a brief, against a rule set, against human-curated reference tokens.</p>
                <p>The workflow is: write a brief, compile it to constraints, run evals across frontier models, and read structured critiques. The linter catches violations before any pixel is locked. The vision critic compares screenshots to intent. Everything is versioned, diffable, and auditable.</p>
