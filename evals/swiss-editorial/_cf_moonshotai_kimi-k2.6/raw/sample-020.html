Artificial Human Design

A framework for evaluating whether a design system was built by humans or generated without taste.

Not another linter that scores green on five metrics and misses the sixth that matters. AHD evaluates the gap between what was specified and what was produced. It checks for coherence, not compliance. It runs under your own keys, on your own hardware, with your own models.

bash 
$ ahd eval-live swiss-editorial \
    --brief briefs/landing.yml \
    --models <specs> \
    --n 10
