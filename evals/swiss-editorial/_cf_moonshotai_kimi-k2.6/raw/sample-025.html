Artificial Human Design

A framework for evaluating design systems, not generating them.

Every AI tool promises to build. AHD is for when you need to judge what was built—by machine or by hand. A brief, a ruleset, and a harness that runs evals against any model you point it at.

bash 
$ ahd eval-live swiss-editorial \
  --brief briefs/landing.yml \
  --models <specs> \
  --n 10

The problem

Generative tools produce surfaces faster than teams can review them. The bottleneck shifted from production to discernment: is this good? Is it consistent? Does it meet constraints that were never spelled out?

AHD treats evaluation as infrastructure. You write a brief in YAML, lint it against 28 rules, and run it through models you control. The output is scored, critiqued, and comparable across runs. Nothing is black-boxed; nothing phones home.
