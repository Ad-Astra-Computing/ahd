      <h1>AHD is a design-evaluation framework, not a generator.</h1>
      <div class="prose">
        <p>It compiles a design brief into a typed specification, runs that specification against frontier models with structured rubrics, and returns scored, attributable criticism. The linter is 28 rules derived from actual failures in human-AI collaboration. The vision critic can be pointed at a rendered DOM or a screenshot. Everything is local-first, auditable, and versioned.</p>
        <p>You already know why this matters. AHD exists to make the evaluation rigorous enough that "AI-generated" stops being a synonym for unconsidered.</p>
