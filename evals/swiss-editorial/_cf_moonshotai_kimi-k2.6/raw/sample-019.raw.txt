  <section>
    <div class="container">
      <p>The tools are abundant. The discernment is not. AHD is a toolchain for designers and engineers who need to know whether generated output meets a standard before it ships — and who need that standard to be legible, versioned, and enforceable.</p>
      <p>It is not a generator. It is not a vibe checker. It is a compiler for design briefs, a linter with 28 rules derived from working systems, and an evaluation harness that runs models against both documents and screenshots.</p>
      <p>You define the brief. AHD compiles it, runs the candidates, and returns scored, annotated output. The vision critic can inspect screenshots if you wire the pipeline. If you do not, the textual linter still runs.</p>
    </div>
  </section>
