      <h1>A framework for evaluating design, not generating it</h1>
      <p class="lead">You have already seen the output: layouts that converge, tokens that overfit, alt text that hallucinates, colour systems that break on real data. AHD is a toolchain that compiles a design brief, runs it against a ruleset, and returns a structured critique. Nothing here generates UI. Everything here judges whether UI meets the terms its authors set.</p>
