Artificial Human Design

\n \n
\n \n

AHD turns a design brief into constraints, runs the output against rules and makes taste inspectable. It treats generated interface work as something to evaluate, not decorate.

\n \n
\n
\n\n \n
\n

Run the eval

\n \n 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
