Artificial Human Design

\n \n

AHD turns taste into runnable constraints. It compiles briefs, checks visible decisions and gives teams a repeatable way to reject generic output before it becomes interface debt.

Run the eval

\n \n

One command runs the brief against the token, models and linter pass. The output is designed for review, not theatre.

\n \n 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
