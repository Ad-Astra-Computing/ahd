AHD

\n\n \n

\n AHD turns design taste into executable checks. It compiles briefs, runs evals and makes model output answer to visible rules before anyone calls it done.\n

\n\n \n
\n 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
