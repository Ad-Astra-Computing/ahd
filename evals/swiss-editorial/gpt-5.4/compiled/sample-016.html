AHD

\n
\n\n \n

\n Artificial Human Design is a framework for checking generated interfaces against real design intent. It compiles the brief, runs the rules, then records where the output held shape and where it collapsed.\n

\n\n \n

\n Built for designers, senior engineers and founders who want a testable design language instead of a lecture.\n

\n\n \n 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
