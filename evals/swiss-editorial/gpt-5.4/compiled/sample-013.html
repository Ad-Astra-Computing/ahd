AHD

\n\n \n

\n AHD turns design taste into executable checks. It compiles a brief, runs a linter, drives model evals and records what passed. The point is not another lecture about generated sites. The point is a framework that can fail a page for real reasons.\n

\n\n \n
\n\n \n
\n 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
