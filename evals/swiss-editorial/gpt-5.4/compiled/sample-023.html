AHD

\n

AHD turns taste into executable checks. It compiles briefs, tests generated interfaces against named rules and separates what works today from what still depends on external systems.

\n
\n\n \n
\n\n \n

Run the eval

\n 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
