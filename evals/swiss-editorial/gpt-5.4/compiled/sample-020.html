AHD

\n\n \n

\n Artificial Human Design is a working system for grading generated interface work against explicit visual rules. It compiles briefs, runs checks, records failures and separates what exists from what still depends on external access.\n

\n\n \n
\n\n \n 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
