AHD

\n \n

AHD turns taste into executable checks. It compiles a brief, runs a rule linter and gives model output a hard surface to fail against. It is for people who already see the problem and want a framework.

\n \n
\n \n
\n
\n\n
\n \n

Run the eval

\n \n 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
