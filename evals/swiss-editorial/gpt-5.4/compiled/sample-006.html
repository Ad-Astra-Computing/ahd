Design evals for generated interfaces

\n

\n AHD turns taste into inspectable constraints. It compiles a brief, runs a linter, records model output and keeps the critique close to the code. No myth. No theatre. Just a stricter loop for people who already know what bad generated design looks like.\n

\n
\n 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
