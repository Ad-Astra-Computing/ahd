AHD

\n\n \n

\n AHD turns design taste into executable pressure. It compiles a brief, checks the result against hard rules and leaves a trace that working teams can inspect. The point is not another lecture about generated pages. The point is a framework that can fail in public.\n

\n\n \n
\n

Run the eval

\n

The token, brief and model matrix are explicit inputs. The output can be read, challenged and repeated.

\n 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
