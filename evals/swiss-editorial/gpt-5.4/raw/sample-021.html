AI output needs a design review it cannot charm.

\n

AHD is a framework for evaluating generated interfaces against briefs, rules, screenshots, and tokens. It treats taste as operational work: explicit, inspectable, and repeatable.

\n

For designers, senior engineers, and founders who already know the problem: AHD gives the team a shared way to reject vague polish, catch patterned slop, and keep human judgment in the loop.
