AHD / Artificial Human Design
\n
Evaluation framework for generated interfaces
\n
\n\n
\n
\n
\n

AHD treats generated design as something to inspect.

\n

\n AHD is a small framework for turning design intent into briefs, lint rules, eval runs,\n and critic passes that can be read, repeated, and argued with.\n

\n

\n It is built for people who already know the failure mode: plausible pages with no taste,\n no accountability, and no memory of the brief. The point is not another lecture. The point\n is a testable loop.\n
