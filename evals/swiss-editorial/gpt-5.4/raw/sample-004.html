Design evaluation for generated interfaces

\n

AHD treats generated design like a thing worth inspecting.

\n

\n AHD is a small framework for turning product briefs into repeatable checks:\n compile the intent, lint the surface, run the eval, and keep the critique close\n enough to change the work.\n

\n Read the code\n
\n\n
\n
\n eval-live\n swiss-editorial\n
\n 
$ ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
