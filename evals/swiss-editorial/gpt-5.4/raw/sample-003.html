Design evaluation for generated interfaces

\n

Taste is operational.

\n

AHD turns design judgement into briefs, rules, tokens, and repeatable evals. It is for teams that already recognize the failure mode and want a framework they can inspect.

\n

Run the same brief across model specs, collect outputs, lint the interface decisions, and leave the subjective argument attached to a visible test surface.
