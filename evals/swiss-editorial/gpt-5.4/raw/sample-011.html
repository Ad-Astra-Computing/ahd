Design evaluation for generated interfaces

\n

Taste is testable.

\n

\n AHD turns a design brief into repeatable checks, screenshots, critique scaffolds,\n and editor feedback for teams that already know generic output when they see it.\n

\n

\n It is a framework for evaluating whether generated UI respects the brief,\n the medium, and the craft constraints designers and engineers normally catch by hand.\n

\n
\n read-the-code\n Framework first. Lecture never.\n
\n
