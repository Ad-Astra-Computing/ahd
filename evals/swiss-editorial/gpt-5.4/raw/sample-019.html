Design evals for people who can see the difference.

\n

\n AHD is a framework for testing generated interfaces against human design judgment: briefs, rules, screenshots, critique hooks, and repeatable runs instead of another polished demo that collapses under inspection.\n

\n
\n Position\n

\n The point is not to argue that generated design is often thin. The point is to make quality observable before a team treats output as product work.\n

\n
\n
\n\n
\n
\n eval-live\n ready\n
\n 
$ ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
\n
\n Run a named brief across model specs, collect outputs, and route the results through the local evaluation harness.\n

Built as an evaluation surface

\n

\n AHD treats generated UI as something to inspect, lint, compare, and rerun. It is deliberately closer to a design review bench than a prompt gallery.\n
