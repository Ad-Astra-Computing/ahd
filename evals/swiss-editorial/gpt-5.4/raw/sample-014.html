Evaluation framework for generated interfaces

\n

Design output deserves a harder gate.

\n

\n AHD turns briefs, lint rules, model runs, and visual critique into a repeatable evaluation loop for people who already know the shape of the problem and want usable pressure on the work.\n

\n\n
\n Premise\n

\n The question is not whether a model can draw a page. It is whether the result survives taste, constraints, implementation review, and the plain reality of being used.\n
