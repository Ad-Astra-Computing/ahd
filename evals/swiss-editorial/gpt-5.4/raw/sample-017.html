Artificial Human Design

\n

Design evals for people who can tell.

\n

\n AHD treats generated interfaces as artifacts to inspect, score, and improve. It gives teams a shared brief language, a rule system, and an evaluation path for the work that normally gets waved through.\n

\n
\n

\n The premise is simple: taste cannot be outsourced, but design behavior can be tested. AHD makes the test visible enough for designers, engineers, and founders to argue about the same object.\n

\n

\n It is not a lecture about synthetic work. It is a framework for catching weak hierarchy, decorative defaults, missing product judgment, and implementation drift before those choices harden into a surface.\n

A framework, not a vibe check.

\n

\n AHD keeps the soft parts inspectable: brief intent, visual rules, model outputs, critique hooks, and token decisions. The goal is a design process where generated work can be accepted, rejected, or revised for legible reasons.\n
