Artificial Human Design

\n

AHD treats generated interface work as something to evaluate, lint, constrain, and inspect before it reaches anyone’s product surface.

\n
\n Position\n

It is not a lecture about taste. It is a small framework for turning design intent into briefs, rules, model runs, critique hooks, and repeatable evidence.
