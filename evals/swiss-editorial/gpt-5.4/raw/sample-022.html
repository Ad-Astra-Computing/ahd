Evaluation before decoration

\n

A framework for judging generated interfaces by the work they do.

\n

AHD treats design output as something that can be briefed, linted, run, criticized, and compared.

\n

It is for people who already know the failure mode: plausible screens, weak product judgment, visual habits repeated until they become noise. AHD makes the critique executable, so a team can argue from artifacts instead of taste alone.

\n
