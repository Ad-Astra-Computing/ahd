A design eval for people who can tell when the page is lying.

\n

AHD turns a brief into constraints, runs generated work against visible standards, and keeps the result inspectable enough for designers, engineers, and founders to argue with it.

\n
\n
\n

Framework, not sermon

\n

No mystique, no taste laundering. Just briefs, rules, harnesses, critics, tokens, and the parts that still require outside infrastructure named plainly.

Run the eval

\n

The live loop is intentionally small: choose a brief, name the model specs, collect ten attempts, inspect the failures.

\n
\n 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
