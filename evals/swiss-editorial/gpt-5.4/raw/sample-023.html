Design evaluation for people who can still tell.

\n

\n AHD turns taste into runnable checks: briefs, lint rules, model evals, critique scaffolds, editor feedback, and tokens that make generated interfaces answer to a standard.\n

\n
\n Premise\n \n The point is not another lecture about bad generated pages. The point is a framework that names the failures, runs the same brief more than once, and leaves an inspectable trail.\n \n
\n
\n\n
\n
\n
\n run the eval\n \n
\n 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
\n
\n

\n One brief, multiple generations, explicit criteria. The result is a design conversation with evidence attached.\n
