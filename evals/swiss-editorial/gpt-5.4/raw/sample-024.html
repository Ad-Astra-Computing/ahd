Design evaluation for generated interfaces

\n

Make the model answer to taste, constraints, and evidence.

\n

AHD is a small framework for judging generated UI by the brief it claims to satisfy: rules, tokens, screenshots, critic passes, and repeatable evals.

\n

It treats design quality as something inspectable. The point is not another lecture about bad output; it is a workflow that makes the failure modes visible enough to fix.

\n
\n\n
\n
\n eval\n bash\n
\n 
ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10
