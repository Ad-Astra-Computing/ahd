  <h1 class="lede">The problem isn't that the machines designed it. The problem is that <em>nobody graded the homework.</em></h1>

  <p class="thesis">You already have the opinion. Last month you saw the same hero four times: the same pill badge, the same two centred CTAs, the same four-column footer, the same gradient where the word "AI" used to be. It wasn't a style — it was a collapse. Models revert to the mean, the mean got scraped, and the mean is now the template.</p>

  <p class="thesis">AHD is a framework for grading that homework. A brief compiler that pins intent. A linter that knows the twenty-eight shapes of slop. An eval harness that runs a live brief against a panel of models and scores the output against a rubric a working designer would actually defend. It is not a generator. It is the adversary the generator needed.</p>
