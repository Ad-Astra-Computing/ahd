  <h1>§01 — premise</h1>
  <p class="lede">You've seen the same landing page forty times this month. Purple gradient, pill badge, three feature cards, a blob. AHD is the <em>eval harness and rule set</em> that treats that pattern as a regression, not a style.</p>

  <p class="body">The problem isn't that models can't produce layouts. It's that the modal output converges on a narrow, legible-to-other-models aesthetic, and that output is now indistinguishable from the training signal. AHD does not fix the model. AHD measures the drift, lints the artifact, and gives a working designer a loop that fails loudly when the thing coming out of the pipe is slop.</p>

  <div class="thesis">
    A framework, not a lecture. A linter, not a lookbook. If you already agree with the thesis, skip to the manifest.
  </div>

  <hr class="rule">

  <h1 id="command">§02 — the one-liner</h1>
  <p class="body">This is the whole surface. Point it at a brief, point it at a set of model specs, and read the diff between what your brief asked for and what the model actually drew.</p>

  <div class="term-head"><span>bash — ~/ahd</span><span>live</span></div>
<pre class="term"><span class="p">$</span> <span class="c">ahd</span> <span class="f">eval-live</span> <span class="f">swiss-editorial</span> <span class="m">\</span>
    <span class="f">--brief</span> <span class="s">briefs/landing.yml</span> <span class="m">\</span>
    <span class="f">--models</span> <span class="s">&lt;specs&gt;</span> <span class="m">\</span>
    <span class="f">--n</span> <span class="s">10</span>
</pre>
  <p class="caption">Runs n=10 generations per model, scores against the 28-rule linter, flags convergence. The brief is authored, not prompted.</p>
