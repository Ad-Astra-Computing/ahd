  <h1>A framework for design that <em>isn't slop</em>, evaluated out loud.</h1>

  <section class="thesis" id="thesis">
    <p class="lede">You've seen the pattern. Centred hero, two CTAs, a pill badge, three feature cards with gradient tiles, a shimmer on the word that sells. The visual grammar of an industry that stopped looking.</p>
    <p class="lede">AHD treats the problem as a measurement problem first and a taste problem second. A compiled brief, a deterministic linter, a harness that runs N rollouts across frontier models on the same spec, a vision critic that scores what actually renders.</p>
    <div class="callout">If a generator will produce a page, the generator should also have to defend it — per rule, per token, per render.</div>
  </section>

    <section id="ships">
      <h2><span>What ships</span><span class="count">today · 8</span></h2>
      <div class="manifest">
        <div class="item"><div class="k">01</div><div class="v"><b>Brief compiler</b><small>YAML → constraint graph. Typed, diffable, reviewable in PRs.</small></div></div>
        <div class="item"><div class="k">02</div><div class="v"><b>28-rule linter</b><small>Deterministic checks: no centred hero+pill+two CTAs, no gradient shimmer on product nouns, no three-card equality, etc.</small></div></div>
        <div class="item"><div class="k">03</div><div class="v"><b>Eval harness</b><small>N rollouts per model, scorecards, seed pinning, diff viewer.</small></div></div>
        <div class="item"><div class="k">04</div><div class="v"><b>Vision-critic scaffold</b><small>Screenshot → rubric pipeline. Offline rubric ships; live critique is gated.</small></div></div>
        <div class="item"><div class="k">05</div><div class="v"><b>MCP server</b><small>Exposes compile, lint, eval, critique as tools to any MCP-aware client.</small></div></div>
        <div class="item"><div class="k">06</div><div class="v"><b>Editor plugins</b><small>In-tree VS Code and Zed extensions, built from the monorepo.</small></div></div>
        <div class="item"><div class="k">07</div><div class="v"><b>Eight tokens</b><small>A deliberately small starter set: ink, paper, rule, accent, mono, serif, measure, rhythm.</small></div></div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section id="gated">
      <h2><span>What is gated</span><span class="count">external · 4</span></h2>
      <div class="manifest">
        <div class="item"><div class="k">g1</div><div class="v"><b>Live frontier-model calls</b><small>Needs your API keys. The harness runs; the rollouts don't until you bring credentials.</small></div></div>
        <div class="item"><div class="k">g2</div><div class="v"><b>Live vision critique</b><small>Needs a multimodal key + a working screenshot pipeline (Playwright on your box, not ours).</small></div></div>
        <div class="item"><div class="k">g3</div><div class="v"><b>Standalone npm packages for editor plugins</b><small>In-tree today. Split-out registries are queued behind signing and publish policy.</small></div></div>

  <section>
    <h2><span>Working assumption</span><span class="count">read once</span></h2>
    <p style="max-width:62ch">AHD is not a design system and not a generator. It is the part in between: a way to describe what a page must be, and a way to fail builds that aren't. Fork it, break it, tell us which rules are wrong. The repo is the artefact; this page is a specimen of it.</p>
