  <h1>A compiler for taste, <span class="amp">not</span> a generator for pages.</h1>

  <p class="deck">
    You already know what the problem looks like. Another month, another
    batch of sites that all share the same five gradients and the same
    rounded-corner feature grid. AHD assumes you've seen it.
  </p>

  <p class="lede">
    AHD is a toolchain for <em>evaluating</em> generated interfaces against
    an explicit design brief — typography, rhythm, hierarchy, restraint —
    instead of vibes. A brief compiles to rules. Rules run as a linter.
    A vision critic looks at the rendered page. Models get scored. You
    get a diff, not a demo.
  </p>

  <p>
    <a class="read" href="https://github.com/ahd/ahd">Read the code <span class="arrow">→</span></a>
    &nbsp;<span style="font-family:'IBM Plex Mono',monospace;font-size:12px;color:var(--dim)">mirror: <a style="color:var(--dim)" href="https://code.ahd.dev/ahd/ahd">forgejo</a></span>
  </p>

    <h2>One command. Ten samples. A score per model.</h2>
    <p class="note">
      Point it at a brief, list the models you want to stress, and get a
      ranked report with linter violations, vision-critic notes, and a
      reproducible seed. No dashboard required.
    </p>
