  <h1>You have seen the slop. AHD is the <span class="kw">evaluation harness</span> that argues with it.</h1>
  <p class="lede">AHD compiles a design brief into rules, lints generated output against those rules, and runs a vision critic over the rendered result. Ten samples, one command, a verdict you can read.</p>
  <p>It is a framework, not a manifesto. The code is the argument. Read it before you agree with it.</p>

  <section>
    <h2>What ships</h2>
    <ul class="manifest">
      <li><span class="tag">today</span><span class="body"><strong>Brief compiler.</strong> Turns a YAML brief into a typed rule set, including must-include and must-avoid constraints.</span></li>
      <li><span class="tag">today</span><span class="body"><strong>28-rule linter.</strong> Static checks for the failure patterns we keep seeing: gradient-text headlines, pill-badge heroes, three equal cards, emoji bullets, trusted-by strips.</span></li>
      <li><span class="tag">today</span><span class="body"><strong>Eval harness.</strong> Runs N samples against a brief, collates pass/fail, emits a diffable report.</span></li>
      <li><span class="tag">today</span><span class="body"><strong>Vision-critic scaffold.</strong> Pluggable critic interface with a deterministic local stub. Bring your own model.</span></li>
      <li><span class="tag">today</span><span class="body"><strong>MCP server.</strong> Exposes the linter and eval harness as tools to any MCP-speaking client.</span></li>
      <li><span class="tag">today</span><span class="body"><strong>Editor plugins.</strong> Source-tree plugins for VS Code, Zed, and Neovim. In-repo, not yet packaged.</span></li>
      <li><span class="tag">today</span><span class="body"><strong>Eight tokens.</strong> A deliberately small starter palette: two typographic scales, four neutrals, two accent roles. CC-BY-4.0.</span></li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>What is gated</h2>
    <ul class="manifest gated">
      <li><span class="tag">ext. keys</span><span class="body"><strong>Live frontier-model calls.</strong> Requires your own API keys. No hosted inference, no proxy, no phone-home.</span></li>
      <li><span class="tag">ext. pipeline</span><span class="body"><strong>Live vision critique.</strong> Requires a multimodal key plus a screenshot pipeline (Playwright or your own).</span></li>
      <li><span class="tag">packaging</span><span class="body"><strong>Standalone npm packages for the editor plugins.</strong> Plugins work from source today; registry publication is tracked in <a href="https://github.com/ahd/ahd/issues/14">#14</a>.</span></li>
      <li><span class="tag">scope</span><span class="body"><strong>Additional tokens.</strong> Extended scales, semantic roles, motion tokens. Scoped after the eight ship stable.</span></li>
    </ul>
    <p class="note">Gated does not mean coming soon. It means the repository does not contain it, and a cost or dependency sits between you and it. Read the issues.</p>
  </section>
