01 / Thesis

A generator that has read everything tends toward the centroid of everything. That centroid is slop.

AHD treats a design token as an executable constraint set: typography, grid, palette, forbidden patterns, required quirks. A brief compiles to a prompt. The output is linted, graded and critiqued against the token. If it drifts to the centroid it fails.

02 / Anchor
Refuse.

Refuse the gradient. Refuse the pill badge with two CTAs. Refuse rounded-2xl on everything. Every token ships its own list of what it will not render, and the linter reads it.

03 / Eval

One command.
Ten runs.
A verdict.

The eval harness renders n candidates against the token, lints each, then asks a vision critic whether the page looks like the brief or like everything else. 

bash$ ahd eval-live swiss-editorial --brief briefs/landing.yml --models <specs> --n 10

Requires a local rules cache and, for live runs, frontier-model credentials. Offline, the harness falls back to fixtures and lints only.

Manifest. What is in the box today and what still needs a key.
