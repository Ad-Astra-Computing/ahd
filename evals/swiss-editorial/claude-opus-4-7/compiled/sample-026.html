Every current model writes the same landing page. Centred hero. Purple gradient. Three equal cards. A testimonial from a logo you have never seen. AHD is a working toolkit for designers and engineers who want the model to produce something else. It compiles a brief into constraints a model can be graded against and then grades ten candidates at once.

§ 02 — One command

Compile the brief. Call n models. Score against the 28 rules. Render the verdicts as a contact sheet.

§ 03
Slop.

You already have the argument. We will not rehearse it. The point is not that the models are stupid. The point is that they regress to the middle of their training data and the middle of their training data is a 2023 SaaS marketing page.

AHD treats this as a tractable problem. Write the constraints down. Compile them. Grade the output. Iterate on the brief, not the vibes.
