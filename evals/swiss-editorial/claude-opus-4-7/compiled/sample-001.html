§00 / Thesis
2026 — 04 — 22

Design/that survives
the model that made it.

AHD is a framework for building interfaces with language models without the house style those models default to. A brief compiler. A linter. A harness that scores ten generations against a rubric. The premise is old: taste is a constraint system. You write the constraints down.

Read before you scroll.
One screen. No pitch.
No pricing. No logos.
AHD
§01

You already know the symptoms. Rounded-2xl on everything. A hero that centres a pill badge above two buttons. Three equal cards with icons in gradient tiles. The shimmer on the word AI. You have seen this page forty times this month.

The cause is not the model. The cause is the absent brief. AHD is the brief, compiled into rules a generator can be graded against.
