01   The thesis

Generative models do not fail at typography because they cannot draw letters. They fail because nobody told them what the page was for. A token is a constraint set. A brief is a compiled constraint set. A linter rejects the ones that drift. A critic scores what is left.

AHD runs that loop on every build. Twenty-eight rules, enforced. One terminal command, reproducible. No vibes.
