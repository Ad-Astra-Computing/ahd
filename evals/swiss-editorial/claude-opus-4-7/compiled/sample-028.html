A framework · not a lecture

Design that refuses to look like everything else a model made this week.

AHD is a compiler, a linter and an evaluation harness for design briefs. It runs frontier models against typographic tokens with strict grammars. It fails the ones that drift into the house style of the feed.

§01 · Thesis

Slop.

The model has a house style. Purple gradients. Rounded-2xl. Three equal feature cards. Pill badge plus two CTAs. You have seen it. AHD treats that house style as a failure mode and writes rules against it. The brief is the spec. The linter is the judge. The token is the grammar.

01 / 04

Run the eval.

One command. Ten generations. Scored against the 28-rule linter and a vision critic. No demo video.

briefs/landing.yml bash 
$ ahd eval-live swiss-editorial \
    --brief briefs/landing.yml \
    --models <specs> \
    --n 10
→ compiling brief ......... ok
→ 28-rule linter ......... 10/10 candidates scored
→ vision-critic .......... 7 pass · 3 fail (house-style drift)
→ artefacts .............. .ahd/runs/2026-04-22T09:14Z/
