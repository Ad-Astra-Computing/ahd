    <div class="num">№ 02 — Thesis</div>
    <h2>Taste&nbsp;is&nbsp;testable.</h2>

    <div class="body">
      <p>
        The problem is not that a model generated the page. The problem is that nobody
        graded it. AHD is a brief compiler, a linter and a judge. It encodes what a
        competent art director would reject at a glance into rules a machine can apply
        before the page ever renders in a browser.
      </p>
      <p>
        Tokens are opinionated. Briefs are explicit about what is forbidden. The linter
        fails builds on rounded-2xl-everywhere, purple-to-blue gradients, centred hero
        stacks and fake trust bars. The vision critic disagrees with you politely and
        in writing.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="aside">
      Not a generator. Not a theme. A harness that sits between a brief and whichever
      model you point it at. Runs offline on fixtures. Runs online when you have keys.
    </div>
  </section>
