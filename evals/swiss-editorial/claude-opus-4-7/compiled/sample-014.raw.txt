  </p>

<!-- rule: thesis — declarative, short sentences, no hedges -->
<section class="thesis grid">
  <div class="label eyebrow">Thesis</div>
  <div class="body">
    <p>Generators optimise for plausibility. Plausibility collapses into a single aesthetic. That aesthetic is the slop you already recognise.</p>
    <p>AHD inverts the loop. A brief is compiled into constraints. Constraints are checked by a linter. Output is scored by a vision critic against the brief that produced it. What survives is not a style. It is a record.</p>
  </div>
  <aside class="aside mono">↳ see §003<br>What ships</aside>
</section>
