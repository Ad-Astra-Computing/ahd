§01Thesis

Taste is a property you can evaluate. Not a vibe you can beg a model for.

A brief compiles to a machine-checkable spec: grid, type, spot colour, forbidden patterns. A linter checks 28 structural rules against the output. A vision-critic reads the render and flags the patterns a linter cannot see. Ten candidates per brief. The worst are killed on sight.
