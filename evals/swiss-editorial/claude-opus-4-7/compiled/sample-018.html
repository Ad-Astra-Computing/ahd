01  —  Position

A framework
against design slop.

You have seen the sites. Purple gradients. Three equal cards. A pill badge above two buttons. AHD is a toolchain that treats taste as an evaluable artefact. Briefs compile. Lints run. Outputs are scored against a token before they reach a human.

02  —  Thesis
One word

Taste.

Generative models collapse toward the median of their training set. The median of web design in 2026 is slop. Prompting harder does not fix this. A style guide does not fix this either. Neither does vibes.

AHD fixes it by making the constraint executable. A token is a specification. A brief is a compiled artefact. A lint is a test. A critic is a second opinion. The one red on this page is the only one permitted by the token.

03  —  Run it

One command.
Ten outputs.
One verdict.

The evaluator compiles the brief, dispatches to the specified models, runs each response through the 28-rule linter, and returns a ranked report. Failed lints are quoted with rule IDs. No rewrites. No opinions from the harness itself.
