§01 / Position Wednesday, 22 April 2026 — Zürich

A framework.
Not a lecture.

You already have the opinion. Most generated sites look the same because they were judged by the same eye. AHD is a small toolkit that forces a different eye: a compiler for briefs, a linter for the obvious mistakes, an evaluation harness that asks several models the same question and reads their answers back against a style token.

Written for designers engineers and founders who have seen enough rounded-2xl gradient tiles for one lifetime.

Open source. Self-host the pipeline.

§02 / The one-liner

Runs the Swiss editorial eval against the landing brief with ten rollouts per model. Output is a scored contact sheet of renders plus a critique JSON.

§03 / The thesis, compressed
Slop.

Not a style. A judgement. It is what happens when a model optimises for plausibility on training data that already regressed to the mean. The fix is not a better prompt. The fix is a written brief a style token a linter and an eval loop that rejects the regression.
